The Lost Child

Support and privacy policy

Support

The Lost Child is a single-player puzzle-platformer for iPhone. If something does not work, or you are stuck, write to tienminh4697@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version.

  • Controls. Move with the stick on the left. Jump and interact with the buttons on the right. Hold interact to grab and pull.
  • Progress. The game saves automatically at every checkpoint. Use Continue on the menu to return to the last one.
  • Stuck on a puzzle. Wait a little: a short hint appears after some time in the same place.
  • Sound. Master, music and effects volume can be changed in Settings on the menu or the pause screen.
  • Starting over. Choose New Game on the menu. Deleting the app also deletes the saved progress.

Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

The Lost Child does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • No accounts. The game has no sign-in and asks for no name, email address or contact details.
  • No tracking or analytics. The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking software.
  • No network use. The game plays fully offline and does not send data to any server.
  • Saved progress. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • No purchases. The game has no in-app purchases.
  • Children. Because nothing is collected, no personal information from children is collected either.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
